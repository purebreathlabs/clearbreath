# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-02-22

ClearBreath is a breathing practice app. This in-app document is an offline copy intended for transparency and accessibility.

## Information We Collect

- Practice data you create in the app (for example, completed breathing sessions and preferences).

## How We Use Information

- To provide and improve core app functionality (for example, showing your streak and practice history).

## Data Storage

- By default, your practice data is stored on your device.
- If you choose to sign in, the app may sync selected practice data to the ClearBreath backend.

## Contact

If you have questions, contact support at: support@clearbreath.life
